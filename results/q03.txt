Q03 | 기본 조회: 최근 완료 주문 5개를 확인한다.
Source: sql/03_queries.sql

SQL:
SELECT order_id, customer_id, ordered_at
FROM cafe_order
WHERE status = 'completed'
ORDER BY ordered_at DESC, order_id DESC
LIMIT 5;
